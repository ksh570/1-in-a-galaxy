# Sleeping Explorers

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 - Abandoned but not Forgotten
Team Fayston

Play here: https://ksh570.github.io/1-in-a-galaxy/

## About

Our team made a mini-game website about NASA missions that are over, like old rovers and probes. We wanted to make learning about them fun instead of just reading text. Everyone researched different missions, and then our leader built the website.

You draw a random explorer, read its card, and play through its mission. When a decision comes up, you pick what you think NASA did. If you get 3 wrong, the mission fails. If you finish, the explorer goes into your collection. There are 17 to collect.

There is also a secret stage. Good luck finding it.

## Explorers

- Moon: Apollo 11, Apollo 12 ALSEP, Apollo 15 Rover, Surveyor 3
- Mars: Viking 1, Viking 2, Sojourner, Spirit, Opportunity, Phoenix, InSight, Ingenuity
- Space: Pioneer 10, Pioneer 11, Dawn, NEAR Shoemaker, Kepler
